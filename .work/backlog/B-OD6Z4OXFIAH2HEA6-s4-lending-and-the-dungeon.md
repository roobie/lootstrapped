# B-OD6Z4OXFIAH2HEA6 S4 Lending and the dungeon

## Why
Lending is where the shop becomes an investor in the parties, and the offscreen dungeon is what makes lent gear matter. Provenance logs turn that into emergent stories at almost no content cost. Provisional: one of the two-week plan's flexible slices. It is reordered, reshaped or replaced by what the playtest gates show, and is next reconsidered at the first playtest gate, after S3.

## Notes
- Intent: lend gear to departing parties; a seeded, deterministic offscreen delve with the supplied gear in its formula, never rendered; item provenance logs.
- About days 9–10 of the plan.
- Lost gear can resurface on someone else's corpse, so you buy back your own sword.
- If the plan slips, the richness of provenance logs is the third thing cut.
