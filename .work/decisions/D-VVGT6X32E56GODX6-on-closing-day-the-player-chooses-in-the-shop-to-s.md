# D-VVGT6X32E56GODX6 On Closing Day the player chooses in the shop to store unsold stock at a cost or dump it

## Context
Storyboard Q11 asked what Closing Day does with unsold stock and the season's last dues.

## Decision
Before the cut to Heaven, the player decides in the shop for each lot: store it at a cost, or dump it.

## Why
The season's last choice stays the player's, in the shop's look, before the board takes over.

## Consequences
Stored stock and its cost are state that carries into the next season, and the board can see them.
