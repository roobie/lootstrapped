# D-3Z4OUYBP2K7FM5VR At night the player restocks tools and reagents and pays dues

## Context
Storyboard Q10 asked whether the night holds anything beyond the tally.

## Decision
After the tally the player restocks tools and reagents and pays dues; there are no upgrades in the first season.

## Why
One small set of choices that feeds the next day, without a second game at night.

## Consequences
Tools have a source and a price in content, and dues are paid as a player action, not deducted silently.
