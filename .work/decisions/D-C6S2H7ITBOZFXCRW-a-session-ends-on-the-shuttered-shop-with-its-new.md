# D-C6S2H7ITBOZFXCRW A session ends on the shuttered shop with its new stickers

## Context
Storyboard Q15 asked whether the session ends on the verdict.

## Decision
After the verdict the session returns to the shuttered shop, its terms stuck on as pastel stickers.

## Why
The return to Ledger Noir closes the hinge (D-AW7FZBF73LOHPLXO) and makes the terms something the player sees.

## Consequences
Terms need a shop-side form (the stickers), which must keep the shop free of percentages (D-XZKESRNAOR3QIXHK).
