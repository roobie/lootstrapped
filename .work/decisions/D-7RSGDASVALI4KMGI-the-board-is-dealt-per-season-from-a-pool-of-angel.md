# D-7RSGDASVALI4KMGI The board is dealt per season from a pool of angel rows

## Context
Storyboard Q12 asked whether the same three angels judge every season.

## Decision
Each season's board is dealt from a pool of angels defined as content rows, each with its mandate.

## Why
Replay value, and angels stay moddable content (D-FSULJNRFYK4Z43SE).

## Consequences
Each angel row has to say which ledger facts feed its mandate, so its judgement is legible (D-G5VL7QCXV5IGX4IY).
