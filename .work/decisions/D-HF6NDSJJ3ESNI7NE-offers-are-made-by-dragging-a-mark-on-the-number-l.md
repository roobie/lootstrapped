# D-HF6NDSJJ3ESNI7NE Offers are made by dragging a mark on the number line; tells stay in the margin until faces arrive

## Context
Storyboard Q6 asked how an offer is entered and where a customer's tells and patience show.

## Decision
The player drags a mark along the number line beside the ink blot. Tells show as notes in the margin; facial expressions on the 3D customer are deferred to v2.0.

## Why
The offer sits next to the uncertainty it is weighed against, with no percentages (D-XZKESRNAOR3QIXHK); faces would cost rigging long before the loop is proven.

## Consequences
The number line is an input as well as a display, and before v2.0 the 3D customer's face shows no expressions.
