# D-FRYXG6INJL354CU2 Before S5, a static low-poly figure stands at the counter

## Context
Customers with clips arrive with S5; earlier slices still need someone at the counter (storyboard Q3).

## Decision
Until then the customer is a static low-poly figure from skelly-assets.

## Why
It shows the real framing and scale from the start, at no animation cost.

## Consequences
Customers carry no animation before S5, so the cold spill, the bell and the margin notes have to tell the player a customer has arrived.
