# D-OMRAYKUI6QF5YFBX Investor messages arrive on ledger events, and the player cannot reply

## Context
Storyboard Q8 asked whether the board speaks during shop days in the first season.

## Decision
Investors send messages during shop days, set off by ledger events; the player cannot answer them.

## Why
The board's pressure is felt every day at the cost of one toast, and it is the only channel that shows the mandates before Closing Day.

## Consequences
Which events trigger which investor is content; messages need a reading of the ledger, not a dialogue system.
