# D-OCI62PALFSLFQQON In the board meeting the player chooses between terms

## Context
Storyboard Q13 asked whether the player acts in the meeting.

## Decision
The player reads the verdict and chooses between the terms offered; no presenting or pleading.

## Why
One real choice keeps the meeting short and still lets the player steer what carries into the next season.

## Consequences
A verdict offers more than one set of terms, and terms are content.
