# D-CHOZUNNOBQ6CITEE One session is one season of about 30 minutes

## Context
The game should give a complete, satisfying run in one sitting, and it ships to itch.io where sessions are short.

## Decision
One session is one season: about 30 wall-clock minutes, roughly 10–12 shop days of 2–3 minutes each, ending at the board meeting.

## Why
"I got my charter renewed" is an accomplishment that fits one sitting. Several seasons per session made a run too long to finish, and a season spread over sessions lost the sense of closure. The exact numbers are a target to refine once it can be played.

## Consequences
The time budget of a shop day bounds how many actions it holds, and so the session length. Session length is a tuning target, not yet a pinned value.
