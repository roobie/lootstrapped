# D-ZP5DV46R5P7JMJL2 The player lights the candle to open the day

## Context
Storyboard panel 1 had to show how a shop day starts against the candle clock (D-34WHPVTJ6MRKUSXM).

## Decision
Each day opens with the candle unlit; the player lights it, and only then does the clock run.

## Why
A calm beat before the clock to read the board's tab and the stock, and the clock stays the player's choice to start.

## Consequences
Planning before lighting is free, so anything the player can do with the candle unlit must not need inspecting.
