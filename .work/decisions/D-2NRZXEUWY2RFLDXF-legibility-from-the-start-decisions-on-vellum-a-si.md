# D-2NRZXEUWY2RFLDXF Legibility from the start: decisions on vellum, a size floor for Fell, a glyph with every trait colour

## Context
Ledger Noir is a mood-first look of darkness, candlelight and a rough old type, and a mood-first look tempts every screen to put words where they are hard to read.

## Decision
Three legibility rules hold from the start. Text the player decides on sits only on vellum. IM Fell has a size floor. Every trait colour is paired with a glyph.

## Why
Noir fails the moment players squint, so the darkness is for mood and the page is for reading. Fell is rough at small sizes, which is charm in a heading and a misread number in a table. The trait colours, verdigris, jelly green and amber, are hard to tell apart for some colour-blind players, so colour must never be the only signal. Retrofitting legibility into a finished look costs far more than designing to it.

## Consequences
Mockups and UI are reviewed against these rules, and a screen that breaks one is wrong whatever its mood. Trait glyphs are part of the woodcut vocabulary.
