# D-IGZD65FTBWFT5FBJ Grim-comic tone

## Context
A pawnshop for adventurers trades in the leftovers of death and failure. The tone decides whether that reads as bleak, as slapstick, or as something in between, and it shapes all writing and art.

## Decision
The tone is grim-comic: being dissolved by a great dungeon jelly is just another day on the job.

## Why
Played straight, the material is depressing; played as pure farce, the stakes vanish. Grim-comic keeps the deaths real enough to matter to the ledger while making them funny, and it suits the investor parody.

## Consequences
Every player-facing text, from item clues to the board's verdicts, is written in this voice. Grim facts stay matter-of-fact rather than tragic or zany.
