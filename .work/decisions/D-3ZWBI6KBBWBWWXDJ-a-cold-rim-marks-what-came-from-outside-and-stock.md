# D-3ZWBI6KBBWBWWXDJ A cold rim marks what came from outside, and stock warms as it ages

## Context
The shop's warm key light is its own; whatever comes in through the door came up from the dungeon, and the player has to judge how far to trust it.

## Decision
A cold rim light marks what came from outside, customers and fresh loot alike, and loot warms over the days it stays in stock.

## Why
Lighting can carry trust before any clue does, which gives the player a reading at a glance and costs no words. Warming over time makes the age of stock visible, and anything still cold after a week becomes a tell in itself.

## Consequences
An item's rim follows from its state, where it came from and how long it has been held, so it stays deterministic under replay (D-2Q5ZKHIVF2C6ZP2C).
