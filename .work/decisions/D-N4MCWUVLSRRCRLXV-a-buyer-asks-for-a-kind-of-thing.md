# D-N4MCWUVLSRRCRLXV A buyer asks for a kind of thing

## Context
Storyboard Q7 asked how a buyer comes to an item.

## Decision
A buyer arrives asking for a kind of thing; the player picks from stock what answers it.

## Why
It makes appraising the stock pay off: knowing what an item really is decides whether it fits the request.

## Consequences
Items need kinds a request can match, as content; the request is written on vellum (D-2NRZXEUWY2RFLDXF).
