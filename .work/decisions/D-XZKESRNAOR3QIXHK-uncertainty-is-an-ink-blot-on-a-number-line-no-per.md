# D-XZKESRNAOR3QIXHK Uncertainty is an ink blot on a number line; no percentages in the shop

## Context
Appraisal yields an estimate, not an answer (D-YX3IUCV2YUN7CT7X), and the shop needs a way to show how much the player knows.

## Decision
An estimate is drawn as an ink blot on a number line, wide and pale when little is known and tight and dark after a tool. No percentage ever appears in the shop.

## Why
A blot shows uncertainty the way a shopkeeper holds it, as a range of belief, and makes the worth of a tool visible as the blot tightens. Percentages promise a precision the shop does not have, and they are Heaven's dialect, so keeping them out holds the border between the two worlds.

## Consequences
The appraisal model has to produce a range, not a single number with a confidence. Percentages are free to appear in Heaven, where they belong.
