# D-AW7FZBF73LOHPLXO Ledger Noir in the shop, Startup Heaven only at Closing Day, joined by one hinge colour

## Context
The player spends nearly the whole session at a candlelit counter and then answers to angels backed by a dragon's fund, so the game has two worlds to draw. D-H574L6YSCOZIDEGL settled where 3D and UI go; this settles how both worlds look. Two moodboard passes explored it, and the owner took the second as the base.

## Decision
The shop is Ledger Noir: candlelight, vellum and ink, set in IM Fell, with woodcut marginalia as texture, and its 3D is low-poly under one warm key light with a slight toon outline. Wyrm Combinator is Startup Heaven, a full SaaS parody played straight, seen only at Closing Day and in investor messages. The two worlds share exactly one hue, candle amber in the shop that is Wyrm orange in Heaven, and their type never crosses the border, except for two deliberate jokes. The way into Heaven is a hard cut through skeleton loaders, never a cross-fade. The guild vacation between seasons gets a third look of its own: the shop with its shutters closed and daylight leaking in.

## Why
The joke is the contrast, and a contrast only lands while it is rare and its borders hold; once either type system leaks into the other, the two looks blur into one muddy style. Played straight, Heaven keeps its horror in its nouns, which is funnier and cheaper than drawing it grotesque. The single shared hue gives the clash a spine: the player works all season by the light of the dragon's fire without being told. A hard cut makes Closing Day an event, and literal skeleton loaders are the one place the two vocabularies agree by accident. The toon-outlined low-poly treatment hides low mesh detail and sits well beside an inked UI; a full ink shader was more distinctive but too risky for the two-week budget. The vacation is neither work nor judgement, so it borrows neither look, and seeing the room in daylight for once marks the season's end.

## Consequences
Every screen belongs to exactly one world and answers to its palette and type. The S0 mockups have to test the outline and the key light against real skelly-assets meshes. Player-facing words in both worlds stay grim-comic (D-IGZD65FTBWFT5FBJ). The moodboard is the reference for the look, not a specification.
