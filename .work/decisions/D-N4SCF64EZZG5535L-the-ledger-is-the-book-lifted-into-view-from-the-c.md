# D-N4SCF64EZZG5535L The ledger is the book lifted into view from the counter

## Context
Every shop panel shows the counter and the ledger together (storyboard Q2); the mockups need the arrangement fixed.

## Decision
The ledger is a book on the counter that the player lifts into view; it is neither a page laid over the scene nor a split screen.

## Why
It stays inside the scene's world and its candle light, and the item stays visible behind it.

## Consequences
The ledger is lit by the scene's light and must stay legible in it (D-2NRZXEUWY2RFLDXF); the mockups test the lifted book against the counter.
