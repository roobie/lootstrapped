# D-UZWR7E6PRMOYIZR5 Lootstrapped, Make Something People Loot, and the Wyrm Combinator board

## Context
The game needs a name for itch.io, a tagline, and a name for the board of investors that runs through every season. The obvious pawnshop-and-adventurers names sit near the "Rogue Trader" trademark.

## Decision
The game is Lootstrapped, its tagline is "Make Something People Loot", and the board of angel investors is Wyrm Combinator.

## Why
The Hacker News and Y Combinator references land with the indie audience on itch.io and make the investor board a running joke from the title onwards. They also stay clear of "Rogue Trader". Names built on pawnshop or loot puns alone said less about the startup-shaped stakes.

## Consequences
Player-facing writing can lean on startup and VC parody. The board's name ties it to a fund, which leaves room for whoever runs Wyrm Combinator (see the dragon bounce in the design).
