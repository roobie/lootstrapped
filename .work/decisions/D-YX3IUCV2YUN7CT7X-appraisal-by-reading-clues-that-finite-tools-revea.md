# D-YX3IUCV2YUN7CT7X Appraisal by reading clues that finite tools reveal; no shopkeeper stats

## Context
Appraisal is the core skill of the shop. The options were reading clues, shopkeeper stats, finite tools, and hired staff (see Appraisal options in the design).

## Decision
The player reads clues, and finite tools reveal them. The shopkeeper has no stats, and time in the shop day is the budget every inspection spends.

## Why
Clues make the knowledge the player's own, which is the most engaging option. Finite tools turn which item to inspect, and with what, into a real decision, and give investor money something to buy. Stats were simplest but left the player passive; staff is a whole extra system, kept as the first extension.

## Consequences
The game needs a vocabulary of clues and must guard against it being "solved". Tool supply becomes a second economy the board funds and audits. Progression comes from tools and the player's learning, never from levelling the shopkeeper.
