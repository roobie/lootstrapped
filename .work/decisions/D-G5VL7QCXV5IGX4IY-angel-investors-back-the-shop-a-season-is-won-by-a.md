# D-G5VL7QCXV5IGX4IY Angel investors back the shop; a season is won by a graded charter renewal

## Context
A shop with no one to answer to has no stakes, and a season with no goal beyond profit is a number to grind.

## Decision
The shop is backed by angel investors who must be pleased, and a season is won when the board renews the charter. Renewal is graded (clean, with terms, or probation) and the terms carry into the next season.

## Why
Someone you owe is the cheapest way to add stakes, and it doubles as a running joke. Profit is only one of the board's concerns, so winning means satisfying people rather than hitting a target, which leaves room for conflicting wishes. A pass-or-fail renewal was simpler but threw away the momentum that grading gives: each season inherits the last one's mess.

## Consequences
The season's verdict comes from the board, so the board's judgement has to be legible to the player. Terms are state that outlives a season.
