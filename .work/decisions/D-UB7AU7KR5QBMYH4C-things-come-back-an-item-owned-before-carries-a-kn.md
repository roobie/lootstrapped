# D-UB7AU7KR5QBMYH4C Things come back: an item owned before carries a knotted thread through its provenance

## Context
Provenance is where the game's emergent stories come from (project design, Provenance), and the strongest of them is an item that comes back to the shop.

## Decision
An item the shop has owned before carries a knotted thread through its provenance, one knot for each return.

## Why
Recognising an item you once held should feel like recognising a face, and a knot makes it visible at a glance, without any text, the moment it crosses the counter again.

## Consequences
Items keep their identity across every transaction. The full mark depends on the provenance logs of S4, though identity is needed from S2.
