ACT I

Day the Seventh, at the counter

The day is one candle long. Everything the player does is a way of spending it.

A day is about two to three minutes (D-CHOZUNNOBQ6CITEE). This one holds a seller, two tools, a haggle, a buyer, a message from Heaven and the tally. Panels 1–10 are the S2 loop; the board only reaches the shop as a pastel tab and a toast.

ACT II

Closing Day: the Wyrm Combinator board

Five days later the candle goes out for the season and the player is cut, without warning, to Heaven.

The last shop day ends like panel 10, and then the hard cut. Everything from here is UI in Startup Heaven's palette and type; no 3D, no Fell, no candle except as a KPI.

FOR THE OWNER

Open questions

Each is something a panel had to show and no decision yet settles. They are left open here on purpose.

What the storyboard could not decide

IN PANEL ORDER · EACH TAGGED WHERE IT ARISES
  1. Q1What opens the day: does the player light the candle, and so choose when the clock starts, or does each day begin already lit? Panel 1.
  2. Q2Where the ledger sits while the counter is in view: a page laid over the scene (drawn here, provisionally), a split screen, or the book lifted into view from the counter. The mockups are where this gets tested. Panel 1 and every shop panel.
  3. Q3Who stands at the counter before S5 brings skelly mobs with clips: a static low-poly figure, a flat cut-out, or only the cold spill, the bell and a hand on the counter? Panel 2.
  4. Q4Is the day phased, sellers in the morning and buyers at midday as the day-loop bounce has it, or one mixed queue? And does passing an hour ring change anything besides the light? Panels 2, 5.
  5. Q5Which form of finiteness each tool has (charges, durability, consumables, or a mix per tool; DESIGN.md's open question), and how a tool's remaining life shows on the counter as opposed to only on the page. Panels 4, 5.
  6. Q6How the player states an offer (writing a figure, dragging on the number line, picking a step), and where a customer's tells and patience show: on the 3D figure, as the note in the margin, or both. Panel 6.
  7. Q7How a buyer comes to an item: asks for a kind of thing, points at the shelf, or is offered something by the player. Panel 7.
  8. Q8Do investor messages arrive during shop days in the first season, what sets one off, and can the player answer? Panel 8.
  9. Q9What happens to a customer, or a haggle, still open when the candle gutters? Panel 9.
  10. Q10Is there anything to do at night beyond the tally (restocking tools and reagents, paying dues, upgrades), and where do tools come from in the first season? Panel 10.
  11. Q11What Closing Day does with unsold stock and the season's last dues (storage at a cost or dumped, as the Closing Day bounce has it), and whether the player does that in the shop before the cut. Panel 11.
  12. Q12How each angel's judgement is made legible: which ledger facts feed which mandate, and is the board the same three every season or dealt per season? Panel 13.
  13. Q13Can the player act in the meeting (present, plead, choose between terms), or only read and accept? Panel 14.
  14. Q14Is probation the floor, or is being fired from your own shop a fourth outcome that ends the run, as the board-meeting bounce has it? Panel 14.
  15. Q15Does a first-season session end on the verdict, or does it show the shuttered shop with its new stickers? Panel 15.
